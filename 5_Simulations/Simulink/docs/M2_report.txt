ROOT CAUSE of the ~20 LSB DC and of the broken LO leakage rejection
==============================================================================
  RTL line  nco_400m_enhanced.v:103
      wire [5:0] lut_index = (quadrant_w[0] ^ quadrant_w[1])
                             ? ~lut_address[5:0] : lut_address[5:0];
  Quarter-wave symmetry needs  quadrant_w[0]  alone. With the XOR the LUT
  index is complemented for quadrants 1 AND 2 instead of 1 AND 3, so in
  quadrants 2 and 3 the sine and cosine outputs are interchanged and the
  complex LO stops being an analytic signal.

  (a) LO waveform error
      constant phase offset (pipeline lag) :  -64.91 deg  (= -0.60 sample)
      RESIDUAL phase error after removing it: rms 37.51 deg, max 151.09 deg
      magnitude |LO| : min 3.238350e+04  max 32767  (ripple 1.17 %)

(b) effective LO DC over the visited phases
      mixer DC for a DC input        : I +12.314, Q -2.198 LSB
      implied LO leakage coefficient : I +0.0481, Q -0.0086  (4.81 % / 0.86 % of FS)
      (an ideal LO would give about -0.5 LSB, i.e. 0.002 % -- pure truncation)

(c) consequence for the 1 MHz scenario -- the input passes straight through
      steady-state mixer DC  +12.314 -> CIC +11.800 -> FIR +20.200
      predicted leakage path : 50 LSB * 512 * 0.0481 = 1231 (mixer)
      expected FIR output rms = 1541  (FIR DC gain 1.7696)
      measured FIR output rms = 1597 LSB
      dominant output spectral line = 1.004 MHz  (input is 1.000 MHz)
      -> CIC stopband rejection (~-76 dB at 119/121 MHz) is bypassed entirely.
